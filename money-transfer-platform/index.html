Case study · SaaS · Mobile · Finance

Money transfers for business, without the friction

A B2B platform where business users manage funds through transfers and currency conversions, track operations from one dashboard, and export reports.

Role
Product Designer
Team
PM, 2 product designers, dev team
Scope
Research, UX, UI, mobile
Platform
Desktop web + mobile

The challenge

How do you give business clients a seamless, transparent way to transfer large sums, competing with global giants while keeping fees low and frustration out?

The company

One of Israel’s leading financial groups, active in insurance, asset and investment management, credit and distribution.

Impact

~25% growth in engagement, ~30% shorter transaction times, and 23% of new users paying in their first session.

01Exploring the problem

A great experience for specific customer needs, under real delivery pressure.

The research was done by another product designer and me, with daily feedback from our product manager, alongside an ongoing development process that demanded a lot of the team’s time.

Market research

We examined B2B money-transfer and currency-exchange platforms such as Currencycloud, OFX and Payoneer to understand how their systems are built and what makes them effective. Building from scratch, we needed to know which features our customers would expect.

02Key insights

Low fees are not enough. It has to be easy to use.

  • Robust admin

    Support operational efficiency and real-time transaction monitoring.

  • Beat the giants

    Combine low fees with high usability as a real alternative to global fintechs.

  • Track everything

    Manage and follow past and pending transactions efficiently.

  • Fit the ecosystem

    Integrate with existing financial systems and compliance requirements.

03User journey

Adding a beneficiary, with validation at every step.

Each step of adding a new beneficiary for future transfers is validated and approved by the back office, to make sure the beneficiary is legitimate.

Open pending requests

Pending requests let the user decide what happens next: requests awaiting authorization can be handed to an admin for final processing, while requests that need no action are view-only.

These are selected use cases of the full system. Feel free to contact me to learn more.

04Wireframing

Hand sketches first, then high-fidelity wireframes.

05Visual design

The company’s brand, tuned for a financial tool.

A minimalistic approach using the company’s UI brand in various shades plus one accent color. We built on the existing colors and fonts, with targeted adjustments for the system’s needs, strengthening the brand’s presence.

The final screens · Desktop

The final screens · Mobile

Translating dense tables and complex flows to a phone.

The mobile app carries over the comprehensive tables and the money-transfer process, the hardest parts of the system to fit on a small screen.
