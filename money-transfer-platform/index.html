Money transfers for business, without the friction

A B2B platform for one of Israel’s leading financial groups, where business clients transfer funds, convert currencies and track every operation from one dashboard.

Role
Product Designer
Team
PM, 2 product designers, dev team
Scope
Research, UX, UI, mobile
Platform
Desktop web + mobile

The challenge

Give business clients a transparent way to move large sums, competing with global giants on both fees and ease of use.

The company

One of Israel’s leading financial groups, active in insurance, investments, credit and distribution.

Impact

~25% more engagement, ~30% faster transactions, and 23% of new users paying in their first session.

01Exploring the problem

Building from scratch meant learning what business clients expect from the best in the market.

Market research

We studied B2B transfer and currency platforms such as Currencycloud, OFX, Tipalti and Payoneer to see how they’re built and which features business clients expect.

02Key insights

Low fees aren’t enough. It has to be easy to use.

Clients needed a real alternative to global fintechs: low fees with high usability, real-time monitoring, clear tracking of every transaction, and a smooth fit with their existing financial systems.
