Case study · SaaS · Admin · Blockchain

An NFT admin platform, built from scratch

A B2B admin system for a heritage organization selling digital collectibles to reach a younger, more diverse audience of donors.

Role
Product Designer
Team
PM, designer, project & dev teams
Scope
Research, UX, wireframes, UI
Platform
Desktop web (B2B)

The challenge

How can a legacy organization launch an emotional, donation-based experience using cutting-edge NFT technology, with no existing reference, and without compromising the spirit of giving?

The company

Israel’s largest organization for historical sites and artifacts, funded by donations, now selling digital items to reach a new generation of donors.

The project

An admin system for item management, donor details, financial reports and donation approval, all run by an internal employee.

01Exploring the problem

We had to create a product from scratch, with no references for admin systems in the NFT world, especially for donations.

We brainstormed before and during research, focusing on admin systems in NFT marketplaces and online stores to understand the hierarchy from collection to item.

Market research

We studied the admin systems of large online-marketplace platforms such as Shopify, Wix, Magento and BigCommerce to learn how they are built and which features work. Since we were starting from zero, this told us where to begin.

02Key insights

Give staff full control, without relying on developers.

  • Own the storefront

    Full control over item uploads and storefront visibility, without developers.

  • Real-time data

    Live data and exportable reports to evaluate engagement and impact.

  • Drops that build hype

    Limited-edition NFT drops to raise anticipation around key dates.

  • Built to scale

    A flexible foundation for future holidays and global donors.

03User journey

One flow in detail: creating collections and items.

The flow guides the user through crafting historical items, categorizing them accurately, and organizing the catalog, collections and items in a clear hierarchy, so the collections stay complete and easy to navigate.

This is one use case of the full system. Feel free to contact me to learn more.

04Wireframing

From hand sketches to high-fidelity wireframes.

After exploring directions in hand sketches, I moved to high-fidelity wireframes of the key screens: the dashboard and the donor purchase completion flow.

05Visual design

Minimal admin, bright NFT world.

With the team and the client, we chose a minimalistic UI built on shades of the company’s primary logo color plus a complementary accent. The marketplace leans into the NFT world: bright colors on a dark-mode base.

The final screens

Representative use cases the internal team performs every day.

06Impact

Non-technical teams now create and manage NFTs at scale, on their own.

  • 27%

    Faster creation

    Time to complete the creation flow

    A step-by-step flow with live previews cut the average completion time (estimated).

  • 30%

    More consistent

    Consistency through templates

    Predefined templates simplified creation and reduced errors (estimated).

  • ~3

    Fewer errors per session

    Fewer data-entry mistakes

    Validations, field restrictions and in-context guidance reduced manual corrections.
