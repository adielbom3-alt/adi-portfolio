Heritage meets blockchain, an admin built from scratch

Israel’s largest heritage organization wanted to reach younger donors with limited-edition digital collectibles. I designed the admin platform that gives their team full control, from the first collection to the final donation.

Role
Product Designer
Team

Scope
Research, UX, wireframes, UI
Platform
Desktop web (B2B)

The challenge

Launch a blockchain donation experience with no existing reference, without losing the spirit of giving.

The company

Israel’s largest heritage organization, funded by donations and now reaching younger donors with digital collectibles.

The project

An admin system for item management, donor details, financial reports and donation approval, all run by an internal employee.

Exploring the problem

We had to create a product from scratch, with no references for admin systems in the blockchain world, especially for donations.

Market research

We studied the admin systems of large online-marketplace platforms such as Shopify, Wix, Magento and BigCommerce to learn how they are built and which features work. Since we were starting from zero, this told us where to begin.

Key insights

What the team needed most was to run it all on their own.

The research pointed to one need above all: independence from developers. Full control over uploads and visibility, real-time reports, and drops timed to key dates, on a foundation built to scale.

User journey

One flow in detail: building collections and items in a clear hierarchy, from draft to published.
