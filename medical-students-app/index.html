Learning that fits between hospital shifts

A mobile-first app for Sheba Medical Center’s interns: daily updates, exams and assignments, and a library of educational videos, all in one place.

Role
Product Designer
Client
Sheba Medical Center
Scope
Research, UX, UI
Platform
Mobile app (B2C)

The challenge

Give interns the learning content they need, fast, on a phone, in the few minutes they have between shifts.

The client

Sheba’s department for interns, which pairs academic learning with practical training in the hospital.

Impact

21% more mobile sessions, rollouts cut from two weeks to 6 days, and 42% faster content discovery.

Exploring the problem

Interns need the right content, on their phone, in the few minutes they have.

Market research

I studied leading learning platforms such as Coursera, Udemy, Domestika and Skillshare to see how the market leaders solve the challenges we faced.

Key insights

Every action had to fit in a few taps.

  • Updates come first

    Real-time notifications from lecturers sit at the top of the home page, so nothing is missed between shifts.

  • Find it fast

    Quick filters by class or lecturer, plus a search with autocomplete, cut the time to the right lesson.

  • One lesson, one place

    Media, reference files and assessments live together on each lesson page, with favorites for quick return.

  • Synced with lecturers

    Assignments and results are saved straight to the lecturers’ admin platform, with no extra steps.
