Learning that fits between hospital shifts

A mobile-first app for Sheba Medical Center’s interns: daily updates, exams and assignments, and a library of educational videos in one place.

Role
Product Designer
Client
Sheba Medical Center
Scope
Research, UX, wireframes, UI
Platform
Mobile app (B2C)

The challenge

Design an intuitive app for people who need important learning content fast. A mobile-first approach makes it even harder.

The client

Sheba’s department for interns builds a framework that pairs academic learning with practical training.

Impact

21% more mobile sessions, rollouts cut from two weeks to 6 days, and 42% faster content discovery.

01Exploring the problem

Interns need the right content, on their phone, in the few minutes they have.

We started from a product brief describing the users and potential use cases, and researched similar apps to analyze their structure and audiences.

Market research

I researched leading learning platforms such as Coursera, Udemy and Domestika, and brought a range of their proven features into our app, to see how the market leaders solve the challenges we faced.

02Wireframing

Home, lessons, assignments: the key use cases in high fidelity.

After exploring directions in hand sketches, I detailed the main flows as high-fidelity wireframes.

03The final screens

Home page

The student’s home page shows the latest and most important notifications, plus processes and actions for their enrolled classes. A filter at the top narrows everything by class or lecturer.

Lesson page

Each lesson is organized into information, videos and uploaded files, plus a section for tests and online assignments that are part of the physical course.

04Key outcomes

Better mobile access, easier navigation, less admin work.
